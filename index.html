Saudi Local Leave Calendar

Ramadan, Hajj, National Day, Foundation Day — auto-apply
Ramadan and Hajj dates follow the Hijri calendar and change every year, so please set these manually. National Day (23 Sept) and Foundation Day (22 Feb) are fixed each year, so defaults are pre-filled. Set the dates and click "Apply to all Active staff" and a leave record will be created automatically for every staff member.
